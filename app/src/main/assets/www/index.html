How to Play

  • Two players or two teams share one screen — left side vs right side.
  • Read the question at the top, then tap the answer on your side. First touch counts.
  • Each side sees the answers in a different order, so copying won’t help.
  • Correct answers pull the rope your way. Wrong answers give the other side a chance to steal (5 seconds).
  • You have 20 seconds per question (25 seconds for ages 10–12). If a team answered, the correct answer is shown before the next one. If nobody answers in time, the question is skipped and comes back later.
  • Pull the other team into the water to win!
Tips for teachers
  • Each team stands on its own side of the tablet.
  • Ages 10–12 has its own 150 simpler questions, including Growing up & puberty instead of sexual health. Ages 13–18 uses the full 300-question teen set.
  • Use Topics to match your lesson. Questions don’t repeat until every question in your chosen topics has been used (until the app is closed).
